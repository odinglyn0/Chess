Process

From broadcast to board in three moves.

No engine to fight, no app to babysit. Relay sits between the live feed and your board.

01 — Connect

Point it at a game

Paste a Chess.com or Lichess game link, or hand Relay a tournament broadcast. It joins the feed the way a spectator would — read-only, nothing installed on either platform.

02 — Read

It reads the move stream

The instant a move is transmitted, Relay parses the notation, resolves the origin and destination squares, and queues the physical motion — usually inside 100 milliseconds.

03 — Move

The board plays it out

Under the surface, an electromagnet rides an X/Y gantry, lines up beneath the correct piece, and drags it along a collision-checked path to its square. Captured pieces get pulled off the edge into an exit channel, out of play.
